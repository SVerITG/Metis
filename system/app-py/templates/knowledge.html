This page used to stack NINE full-width sections vertically: search, semantic search, slipcases, index cards, background layers, the Zotero browser, the knowledge corpus, PDF full-text search, topic coverage and the coverage-gap detector. Two things were wrong with that, and they compounded: · IT COULD NOT BE READ. the researcher's words: "at the bottom there were several very long sessions… not long lists of documents or items". A reference surface is consulted with a purpose — you arrive wanting to review what is new, OR to find something you own, OR to check where your coverage is thin. Stacking all three means every visit scrolls past the other two. · EVERY SECTION FETCHED ON ARRIVAL. Nine hx-trigger="load" blocks, several of them heavy, all firing before the first one had rendered. So the sections are grouped by the QUESTION they answer, and only the open tab fetches. The grouping is deliberate: New — what appeared since I last looked (the thing with a deadline) Catalogue — what do I already have (search + browse) Shelf — the books, as objects (clickable, openable) Corpus — what is indexed and searchable full-text (backgrounds, PDFs) Landscape — where am I thin (coverage, gaps) Deferral uses a custom `loadtab` event rather than hx-trigger="load", because "load" fires for hidden elements too — tabs would have hidden the scrollbar while keeping all nine requests. ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ #} {% block content %}

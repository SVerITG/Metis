{#- _item.html — ONE list row, and ONE action bar, for the whole application. Rebuilt 2026-08-26 after a measured design audit: 190 templates carried 3,166 inline `style=` attributes with 12,040 hand-written declarations — 52 font sizes, 222 paddings, 60 button class combinations, and NINE different shapes for "a thing in a list". The triage row added earlier that day became the sixtieth button variant, which is precisely why it read as bolted on. WHY THE BUTTONS WERE IN THE WRONG PLACE. They were four text pills flowing after the summary, so their x-position depended on how long the summary was — a different spot on every row. The item is now a three-column grid (media · body · actions) and the bar sits in the actions column, which puts it at the same place on every row of every list, whatever the content does. ICONS, NOT LABELS. Five words repeated across sixty rows is three hundred words of chrome competing with the headlines the page exists to show. Each button keeps a real 30px target, a `title`, and an `aria-label`, so nothing is lost but the visual noise. -#} {#- One per icon.

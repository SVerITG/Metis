# Public Health / Global Health — the reading behind the expertise

**What this is.** The domains an MPH and a global-health specialisation actually cover, and
for each one the books you would have to read to master it. This is the *source*; the pack
manifest (`pack.json`) is *derived* from it.

**Why it exists.** A background layer that is assembled from whatever happened to be
downloadable will look complete and be lopsided — the first version of this layer was
eight European health-systems reports and called itself a public health foundation. Writing
the curriculum first makes the gaps visible instead of invisible.

**Status key**

| | meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | in the layer now |
| 🔓 | open access — free to fetch, goes in `pack.json` as a URL |
| 🔒 | licensed — named in `pack.json` as `access: "manual"`, needs an institutional login |

Licensed entries are not a failure. Most fields have one or two texts that are simply the
standard, and a curriculum that omits them to stay downloadable is lying about the field.
